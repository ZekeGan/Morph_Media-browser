This applies to the name "M:orph" (with the subtitle "Media Browser"), the mark (a blocky M with a colon in its notch), the app icon, and anything that looks confusingly similar. The artwork lives in `docs/brand/`, `build/icon.ico`, `build/icon.png` and `build/icons/`, and the mark is drawn in `src/BrandMark.tsx` (the code follows LICENSE; use of the mark follows this policy).

## You may, without asking

- Use, study, modify and share the code under [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- Say what your work is made from, in plain words: "based on M:orph", "a fork of M:orph".
- Show the name and the logo when you write about, review, link to or teach M:orph, and in screenshots of the official application.
- Share **unmodified** official builds free of charge with their name and icon. Paid redistribution also requires permission under [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## You may not, without written permission

- Publish a modified version under the name M:orph, or with the M:orph logo or a confusingly similar one. That includes a version that changes only the logo, or only the name.
- Suggest that your version, product, website or service is the official one, or is made, approved or endorsed by the author.
- Use the name or the logo as the name of your product, package, repository, domain, app store listing or social media account.
- Present a copy as the official product, or suggest that the author makes, sells, supports or endorses it, when that is not so. Sales must also comply with [LICENSE](LICENSE); permission to sell a modified version does not grant use of the official name or logo.

## Official builds

The official builds are the ones published on this repository's Releases page. A copy from anywhere else is not official, even when its code is unmodified. If you redistribute an unmodified official build, say plainly that it is a third-party copy and where the official build can be downloaded.

## If you publish a modified version

1. Give it **its own name and its own logo** (not just a recoloured or re-cropped copy of ours), and replace the files listed above.
2. Keep the copyright notices, [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md), and provide recipients with the corresponding source code under [LICENSE](LICENSE).
3. State clearly that it is a modified version and not the official M:orph. A line such as "Based on M:orph by Zeke Gan" is welcome and allowed.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue in the repository.
